# Panagiotis Kiousis

Electrical and Computer Engineering student at the University of Patras, currently completing a master's thesis at TU Graz (Institute of Visual Computing).

My research focuses on event cameras and LiDAR-based localization. The thesis, *Learning Which Correspondences to Trust: Confidence-Weighted Event-Camera Localization in LiDAR Maps*, led to a paper submitted to ICRA 2027.

**Interests:** computer vision (especially 3D vision), 3D computational geometry, machine learning, computer graphics, robotics and reinforcement learning.

I am a research assistant in the VVR (Visualization and Virtual Reality) Group at the University of Patras, working on the EU-funded DIDYMOS-XR project, and I have worked on biomedical computer science projects in collaboration with Prof. Athanasios Lourbopoulos.
